Subject: Report of the Vigilance & Enforcement wing on suspected cartelisation in drain works contracts in three Urban Local Bodies, 2023–25.
1. The Vigilance & Enforcement wing undertook a confidential analysis of 46 drain works contracts awarded during 2023–25 in three Urban Local Bodies.
2. In 31 of the 46 contracts, the same four bidders participated, and the lowest bid was within 0.5% of the estimate in 27 cases, indicating possible bid rotation.
3. The wing recommends (i) referral of the matter for detailed investigation, (ii) mandatory e-procurement analytics for works above Rs.25 lakhs, and (iii) rotation of officers handling tender evaluation.
4. This report is for the information of the Secretary to Government only and shall not be circulated.
