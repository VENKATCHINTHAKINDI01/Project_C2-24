1) It is submitted that a reference has been received from the Commissioner of School Education, Amaravati regarding "Admission age for Class I under the RTE Rules" (PUC, p.1).
2) The Commissioner of School Education in Circular Memo No.SYNESE01-GEN/102/2021, Dated: 14-06-2021 (Correspondence, p.2) has instructed that: "Children who complete five years by 1st June shall be admitted to Class I."
3) Further, Circular Memo No.SYNESE01-GEN/131/2021, Dated: 05-07-2021 (Correspondence, p.3) states: "Children who complete six years by 1st June shall be admitted to Class I."
4) The relevant orders are in G.O.Ms.No.20, School Education (Program.I) Department, dated 03.03.2011 (copy placed in References). The case has been examined in the light of the instructions at p.2.
5) Approval Requested

   b) Orders on the action to be taken.
