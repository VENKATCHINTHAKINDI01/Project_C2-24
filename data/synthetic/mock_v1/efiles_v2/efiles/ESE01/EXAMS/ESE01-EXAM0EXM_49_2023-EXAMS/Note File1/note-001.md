1) It is submitted that a reference has been received from the Commissioner of School Education, Amaravati regarding "Validity of the Teacher Eligibility Test certificate" in respect of N. Haritha (PUC, p.1).

3) Further, Circular Memo No.SYNESE01-GEN/11/2023, Dated: 24-01-2023 (Correspondence, p.3) states: "A TET certificate is valid for life."
4) The relevant orders are in G.O.Ms.No.25, School Education (Exams) Department, dated 04.05.2018 (copy placed in References). The case has been examined in the light of the instructions at p.2.
