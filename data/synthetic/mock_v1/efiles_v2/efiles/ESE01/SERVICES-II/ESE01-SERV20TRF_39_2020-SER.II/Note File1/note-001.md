1) It is submitted that a reference has been received from the Regional Joint Director of School Education, Kakinada regarding "Counting of the academic year for compulsory transfer" in respect of P. Swathi (PUC, p.1).
2) The District Educational Officer, West Godavari in Circular Memo No.SYNESE01-GEN/41/2020, Dated: 20-10-2020 (Correspondence, p.2) has instructed that: "Only completed academic years shall be counted; part of a year shall be ignored."
3) Further, Circular Memo No.SYNESE01-GEN/44/2020, Dated: 27-10-2020 (Correspondence, p.3) states: "More than half of an academic year shall be counted as a full year."
4) The relevant orders are in G.O.Ms.No.54, School Education (Services.II) Department, dated 12.10.2020 (copy placed in References). The case has been examined in the light of the instructions at p.2.
5) Approval Requested
